WHAT IS THIS?

A little duck with a big purpose.

I’m a Traveling Duck on a mission to spread kindness, positivity and collect memories all across Michigan!

Each person becomes one small part of the duck’s story. Take it somewhere you love, somewhere that inspires you, or somewhere that holds a special memory—then pass the adventure on.
